## About me

I am a machine learning and computer vision engineer working on real-time industrial vision systems—from camera data and dataset development to model training, system integration, and deployment on customer hardware.

My current work includes object detection, classification, and tracking for automated sorting and manufacturing quality control. I have deployed multi-camera systems with up to 10 cameras, worked with datasets containing several hundred thousand images, and delivered inference pipelines sustaining 30 FPS on production hardware.

My earlier experience in embedded systems, industrial electronics, and ASIC physical design helps me approach vision as a complete system: sensors, compute hardware, software, and machinery must all work reliably together.

I am especially interested in **robotics perception** and intelligent automation. I am currently deepening my experience in camera calibration, 3D vision, RGB-D perception, pose estimation, ROS 2, and edge deployment for robotic systems.

## Technical focus

- **Computer vision:** object detection, classification, tracking, dataset curation, augmentation, model evaluation, and error analysis
- **ML and inference:** Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, YOLO, EfficientNet, SAM, ONNX, and TensorRT
- **Production systems:** industrial camera SDKs, multi-camera pipelines, NVENC, FFmpeg, TCP/PLC integration, Linux, Docker, and Git
