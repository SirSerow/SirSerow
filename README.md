## A little about me

I am a machine vision engineer who enjoys building complete systems—from collecting camera data and training models to running inference on real hardware.

What interests me most is **robotic perception**: giving robots the ability to see, understand space, and act reliably outside controlled demos. I am exploring camera calibration, depth and 3D vision, object pose estimation, ROS 2, and efficient edge inference.

I started closer to hardware, working with embedded systems, electronics, and ASIC design. That background still shapes how I approach vision projects: the model is only one part of a system that also includes cameras, compute constraints, communication, and physical machinery.

## Things I enjoy exploring

- 🤖 Robotics perception for manipulation, inspection, and autonomous machines
- 👁️ Object detection, classification, tracking, and data-centric computer vision
- 📐 Camera geometry, calibration, depth, point clouds, and pose estimation
- ⚡ Fast inference on Raspberry Pi, Jetson, and other edge devices
- 🔧 Hardware–software integration and practical industrial automation
- 🧪 Reproducible experiments comparing modern ML with classical vision
